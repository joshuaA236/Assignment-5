(3) What I notice is that the mmap finished almost 130 seconds faster than the normal runs. Looking at the graph, mmap finished around 15 seconds and the normal run finished around 150 seconds. Also, mmap reached around 590,000 KB while the normal run hit about 400,000 KB. As we learned this week, mmaping avoids manually reading entire files into separate allocated buffer. Instead, it allows the OS to load pages as needed. For the normal runs, this doesn't happen. Instead, the image is loaded seperately for each process.   
(4) Minor page faults happen when a process needs a page that is not mapped into a page table, but the data is avaliable in physical memory. A major page fault happens when the page that is needed  is not in physical memory and the OS then has to call the disk to load it.
